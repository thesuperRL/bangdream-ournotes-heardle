• BANG DREAM! OUR NOTES CARDOKU

Every card title, every card attribute and every piece of card art in this game comes from haneoka, a fan-run database for BanG Dream! Our Notes. The school and school year clues come from the BanG Dream! Wiki. The cards and artwork are the property of their rights holders (Bushiroad, Craft Egg and Ninian). Nothing here is sold or monetised; the thumbnails are used for identification in a fan puzzle game and are stored downscaled. The format is modelled on amiya.io/arkdoku. If you own one of these images and want it removed, DM thesuperRL on Discord and it will be taken down.
